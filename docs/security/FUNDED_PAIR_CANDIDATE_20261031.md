use height 31; the NFT **collection** uses 30, Native uses 32, and U124 FT uses

than another hardcoded market tuple. Code, layout, function root, height, field

lifecycle route recognize the distinct profile. SCAN/CARRY retain the same

method, not the FT market's `activate_batch_vault`.
The returned profile and supported-market list are both immutable. Stage and
registration routes also require the policy's reviewed market kind/profile;
another market label under the same key family cannot substitute for it.

All routes retain actual ordered calls, callers, parameter hashes/counts,

still required; the lifecycle stops at `awaiting-wallet` without it.

## Preserved failures and bounded correction

The first compiler attempt produced Book bytecode but rejected NFT's invalid
`native_view` field. NFT has its distinct `external_view` identity. Only that
application generator selection was corrected; Book was reused by exact source,
configuration, compiler and artifact SHA256. There were three compiler attempts
and two successful new artifacts, with all original output preserved.

Two VM attempts exposed an acceptance-driver sequencing error. `ack_delivery`
cleans the **outgoing NFT delivery receipt after its recipient claims**; it is
not an incoming-deposit acknowledgement. The corrected path retains both the
"nothing to acknowledge" and "fee belongs to active order" guards. It claims
the exact token/metadata before the SDK receipt acknowledgement. No market
predicate was removed to make the test pass.

The first complete proof cohort accepted seven Book UPSs, then rejected the
second OPEN. Its planner inherited the previous batch's terminal cursor 2 while
the new source had one member. The triangular circuit correctly rejected the
negative `total - settled` range. New OPEN and inactive maintenance requests now
use zero frontier settlement cursor; native and formal host preflight reject
stale cursors before CFC proving. This is a request-planning correction, with
the spending circuit and market bytes unchanged.

The corrected continuation must independently re-verify the seven original
exact requests, full Endcaps, key proofs, actual ordered stack, signature domain,
source/current roots, starting nonce/state and independently replayed VM final
state. It never re-signs, broadcasts or trusts a saved success flag. Original
requests and failed outputs stay intact. The original 28-unique-UPS, 2,400-second
aggregate proving-process and 8-GiB sampled-family-RSS ceilings remain; at most
21 new complete UPSs may be generated by the bounded continuation.
